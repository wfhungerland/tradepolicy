# Government expenditure by function (script 86, 2026-11-11)

Eurostat COFOG (gov_10a_exp), Germany, total expenditure 1995-2024; general government
(consolidated) and the four subsectors (federal, Länder, municipalities, social security);
second-level groups for general government from 2000. Real values with the GDP deflator
(2024 prices), per head with average population. Site tab "Staatsausgaben".

- 2024: general government spent 2,140 bn EUR (48.8 % of GDP); social protection 41 %,
  health 15 %, administration and interest 13 %, economy and transport 11 %, education 9 %,
  defence 2.7 %.
- Real spending per head 2019-2024: social protection +548 EUR, economy and transport +418,
  administration and interest +390, health +211, defence +150, education +90.
- Subsector values include transfers between levels; their sum exceeds the consolidated
  total by 14-24 % (1995-2024), so levels are shown side by side, never added.
- 1995 includes the federal takeover of the Treuhand debt (about 130 bn EUR, economic affairs).
